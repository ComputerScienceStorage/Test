## probate-back-office

### Implementation changes

#### Mandatory

- `ccdImports/configFiles/CCD_Probate_Backoffice/ComplexTypes.json:32-35` (`ApplicantFamilyDetails` half-blood niece/nephew parent fields): Replace `relationshipToDeceased="NeverShowThisField"` with `relationshipToDeceased="halfBloodNieceOrNephew"` for the parent-death and parent-adopted-in fields; add `AND halfNieceOrNephewParentAdoptedIn="Yes"` to the adoption-location field and `AND halfNieceOrNephewParentAdoptedIn="No"` to the adopted-out field so Caseworker XUI displays only the applicable saved answers.

- `ccdImports/configFiles/CCD_Probate_Backoffice/ComplexTypes.json:43-46` (`ApplicantFamilyDetails` whole-blood niece/nephew parent fields): Replace `relationshipToDeceased="NeverShowThisField"` with `relationshipToDeceased="wholeBloodNieceOrNephew"` for the parent-death and parent-adopted-in fields; add `AND wholeNieceOrNephewParentAdoptedIn="Yes"` to the adoption-location field and `AND wholeNieceOrNephewParentAdoptedIn="No"` to the adopted-out field so Caseworker XUI displays only the applicable saved answers.

- `ccdImports/configFiles/CCD_Probate_Backoffice/ComplexTypes.json:31,42` (`halfBloodSiblingDiedBeforeDeceased` and `wholeBloodSiblingDiedBeforeDeceased`): Remove their niece/nephew Caseworker display conditions when the dedicated parent-death fields become visible, so XUI does not show two parent-death questions for one co-applicant.
